# Client-Facing Assets

## Fit-call agenda — 30 minutes

1. What business process is affected and what happens if it fails?
2. Is the platform K2, Nintex, Odoo, or an integration around it?
3. What changed, what is blocked, and who owns the outcome?
4. What environments, access paths, documentation, and internal expertise exist?
5. What is the desired decision: repair, upgrade, migration plan, ongoing support, or another route?
6. Is a paid Stability Audit the right next step?

**Close:** “Based on this conversation, I recommend a Stability Audit. It produces a risk register, prioritized roadmap, and a defined next-step proposal. I will send a fixed-scope quote; no production changes are included unless we agree to them in writing.”

## Stability Audit — scope statement

**Objective:** Establish a fact-based view of the platform’s current state, material risks, supportability, and safest next actions.

**Included:**

- One kickoff and up to three stakeholder interviews
- Review of available architecture, environment, workflow/customization, integration, incident, and release information
- Backlog and support-process review
- Risk register categorized by operational impact and urgency
- 30/60/90-day prioritized roadmap
- Executive readout and written findings

**Excluded unless added in writing:** Production changes, code remediation, after-hours coverage, vendor fees, license purchases, broad penetration testing, data migration, and unlimited discovery.

**Client responsibilities:** Name a business and technical owner; provide legal access to agreed materials; make timely decisions; protect credentials; and identify any regulated or sensitive data restrictions before sharing information.

**Commercial terms:** Fixed fee of $[amount], invoiced and paid before kickoff. Target is approximately 10 consulting hours. Any change to scope requires written approval.

## Recovery Sprint — proposal skeleton

| Section | What to state |
|---|---|
| Business outcome | The measurable condition that will be true at acceptance |
| Scope | Systems, modules/workflows, environments, and tasks included |
| Assumptions | Access, client decisions, data quality, vendor participation, test resources |
| Deliverables | Code/configuration, documents, release notes, test evidence, handoff |
| Exclusions | Everything not required for the named outcome |
| Acceptance | Who signs off, test conditions, acceptance date |
| Change control | Written scope/rate/budget impact before work begins |
| Pricing | Fixed milestone price or rate with not-to-exceed cap |
| Risk | Production, data, security, schedule, and dependency risks |

## Audit-readout structure

1. Executive summary: what is at risk and why it matters
2. Current-state snapshot: people, environments, workflow/customization, integrations, and support process
3. Top five risks: impact, evidence, owner, recommended action
4. 30-day stabilization plan
5. 60–90 day modernization or upgrade/migration plan
6. Recommended Recovery Sprint: exact outcome, assumptions, scope, cost, and decision needed
7. What the client should own internally next
